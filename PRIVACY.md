# Sentinelstrap for Roblox: privacy policy

Sentinelstrap for Roblox is a browser extension that adds tools to roblox.com and connects to the Sentinelstrap app on the same computer.

**What it reads.** On roblox.com it reads Roblox's own public information (games, servers, profiles, items, your friends and trades) using your existing Roblox session, the same way the Roblox page itself does. It never reads or stores your password or your Roblox login cookie.

**Where data goes.** Nowhere outside your computer, with two exceptions you control:
- Roblox: requests go to Roblox's own servers (roblox.com), as the site does.
- Rolimons: only if you turn on "Item values", the extension downloads Rolimons' public item list from api.rolimons.com. Nothing about you is included in that request.

**What it handles, on your computer only.** Roblox names (yours, your friends', and your saved accounts' names from the Sentinelstrap app), your Robux balance and transaction totals and trade values (shown on your transactions and trades pages), the Roblox pages you recently viewed, and Roblox page content such as games, servers, inventories and trades. This is shown to you and, where noted below, kept in your browser; none of it is sent to the extension's developer or anyone else.

**What it keeps.** Your feature switches, theme and panel settings are saved in your browser (and synced by your browser if you use browser sync). Notes, pinned friends, last-seen times, recently viewed pages and the unfriend list are kept in this browser only.

**The Sentinelstrap app.** The extension talks to the Sentinelstrap app on your PC through the browser's native messaging. The app can tell the extension your saved accounts' names (never their login data), your local playtime and saved games, and it launches Roblox when you press Play or Join.

**Actions on your account.** The extension never buys, trades, chats or changes settings for you. The only account actions are Decline and Cancel on the trades page, and they happen only after you click and confirm.

**No tracking.** No analytics, ads or selling of data.
